The hacker's wallet holds a diversified portfolio of stolen assets, with bitcoin and ether making up a large portion, and the $2.38 billion frozen by Circle and Tether represents only a fraction of the total. Binance's SAFU fund, which was established in 2018 and is funded by 10% of trading fees, is reportedly only about $1 billion in BTC, meaning it is far too small to cover the losses.

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weight speech-augmented language model (SALM) that transcribes 40 languages with verbatim or written-form output, speaker attribution, word timestamps, and speech translation, all controlled via natural language instructions. Alongside it, NVIDIA introduced Granary v2, an updated multilingual speech dataset and curation system that adds aligned transcript variants and instruction–target pairs for multi-task speech-language training. This release advances multilingual speech AI by offering a single promptable model that adapts to different transcription styles and tasks, reducing the need for separate specialized models. The accompanying Granary v2 dataset provides open resources for fine-tuning and research, which could accelerate development for low-resource languages and improve accessibility of speech technology. Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is a 30B mixture-of-experts model with about 4B active parameters, capable of processing recordings up to one hour long. Granary v2 extends the original Granary dataset (which covered 25 European languages) with aligned transcript variants and instruction–target pairs, enabling multi-task training.

**Background**: Automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems traditionally require separate models for different tasks like transcription, translation, or speaker diarization. Recent advances use large language models augmented with speech encoders (SALMs) to unify these tasks via prompting. NVIDIA's Nemotron family is a series of open models, and Granary is a large-scale multilingual speech dataset first released in 2025 to address data scarcity for low-resource languages.
