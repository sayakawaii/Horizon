**Results:** Evaluation of 9 leading models reveals that the primary bottleneck lies in disentanglement and attribute binding rather than scene-level composition, with even the best model scoring only 0.74 on attribute fidelity.

**Significance:** TRACE-Bench provides a systematic and diagnostic benchmark for multi-reference image generation, offering insights that holistic scoring misses and guiding future improvements in model capabilities.

**Problem:** RAW video restoration is essential for low-level perception, but binary neural networks (BNNs) struggle with temporal coherence and activation distribution modeling, limiting their effectiveness in video tasks.

**Method:** The paper proposes BinRVR, a binarized RAW video restoration framework. It introduces a Binarized Information Interaction Module (BIIM) for unified spatial-temporal modeling and a Distribution-Aware Binarized Convolution (DAB-Conv) that uses full-precision activation statistics to reduce quantization errors. **Significance:** This work advances efficient video restoration by extending binarization to RAW video with minimal performance loss, enabling deployment on resource-constrained devices.
