Liver regeneration occurs through compensatory hyperplasia of hepatocytes, involving priming, proliferation, and termination phases triggered within hours of partial hepatectomy. **Background**: The liver is the only internal human organ capable of natural regeneration of lost tissue; as little as 25% of a liver can regenerate into a full-sized organ. This regeneration is not true regrowth of the same shape but rather compensatory growth to restore functional mass. Other organs like the heart and kidneys have very limited regenerative capacity, leading to permanent damage after injury or disease.
